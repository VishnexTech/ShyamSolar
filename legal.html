PRIVACY POLICY

How we handle your information.

Enquiry information. If you contact Shyam Solar System or submit the enquiry form, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, solution of interest, and project details. The form opens your email application with these details addressed to our team; this website does not submit the form to a server.

How it is used. Information you send is used to respond to your enquiry, discuss a potential solar project, and follow up about the services you asked about. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information through the enquiry form.

Third-party resources. This website loads fonts from Google Fonts and illustrative photographs from Unsplash. When your browser requests those resources, the providers may receive technical information such as your IP address according to their own policies.

Questions or requests. For privacy questions or a request concerning information you have emailed us, contact info@shyamsolarsystems.com.

TERMS & CONDITIONS

Using this website.

General information. Website content is provided for general information about solar solutions and Shyam Solar System. It is not a final technical design, offer, or service agreement.

Calculator and savings. Calculator results are illustrative estimates based on the values entered and simplified assumptions. They are not a promise of system output, savings, payback, subsidy eligibility, or utility approval. Actual outcomes depend on the site, equipment, energy use, tariffs, local rules, and other factors.

Images and examples. Some photographs are illustrative placeholders and do not necessarily show completed Shyam Solar System projects. Product warranties and installation scope are governed by the applicable manufacturer documents and written project proposal.

External websites. Links to third-party websites are provided for convenience. Shyam Solar System does not control their content or policies.
